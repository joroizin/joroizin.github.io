Final scores, but make it a ceremony 👑

Today the finale lands all at once: crown, champion name, scoreboard and the Play-again button appear inside one second, then the confetti cannons run for eight. Cheerful — but there's no build-up, and the room never gets its collective gasp. Below are five auto-running ceremonies (~7–11s, big screen only, using only data the finish screen already has). Timings shown are suggestions, everything is tunable.

Each stage plays when it scrolls into view — hit ↻ replay to watch again. Sound cues are noted per option (a static file can't play the real ones).

Option A — Podium Build

~9s award-show classic

The field settles in first as a chip row, then the podium builds itself: bronze rises… silver rises… the lights go down, a spotlight hits the empty gold block under a drumroll — and the champion slams onto the top step with a crown drop and confetti.

  • Beats: 0s scores-are-in → 1s field chips → 2s third place → 4s second place → 6s dim + drumroll on the empty gold block → 7.5s champion slam.
  • Sound: soft riser under the announcements, drumroll through the dim, the existing reveal sting + a crowd pop on the slam (rides lib/sound.ts cues like the round podium).
  • Tie: co-champions share the gold block, cards side by side — same "equal respect" rule as RoundPodium.
  • 20 players: the field row wraps to two lines of chips; the podium is unchanged.
  • Build: new component; CSS height transitions + the reveal-timeline pattern already used by RoundPodium.

Option B — Spotlight Roulette

~9s max tension

Every player's name is on stage, dimmed. The spotlight jumps from name to name, fast at first, then slower… and slower… drumroll building — until it lands. The stage clears, golden rays spin up, and the champion's name fills the screen. The full standings slide in along the bottom.

  • Beats: 0.5s chips pop in → 1.5s roulette starts (hop every 150ms) → decelerates over ~5s with the shake building → 7s landing: flash, rays, crown, count-up → 8.5s bottom standings strip.
  • Sound: a tick per spotlight hop (accelerando in reverse), drumroll layering in, cymbal + fanfare on the landing.
  • Fairness note: the hop order is fixed and the landing is predetermined — pure theatre, no RNG in the result.
  • Tie: the beam splits and lands on both names at once; both slam center together.
  • 20 players: chips shrink a size and wrap — a denser wall actually makes the roulette better.

Option C — Score Race

~7s photo finish

Every player's bar sprints from zero at the gun. The pacing is choreographed so the top two run neck-and-neck until the last half-second, when the champion surges past at the line — crown pops at the bar tip, medals stamp in. The whole room watches their own bar the entire time.

  • Beats: 0.5s rows appear → 1s the race (4s, every score counting up live) → 5.5s photo-finish flash, crown, medals stamp down the ranks.
  • Sound: starting gun / whoosh at the launch, rising synth through the race, sting at the finish.
  • Honesty note: the "overtake" is easing theatre (the mock also keeps rows in final order; the real build would reorder rows live as bars cross). A true per-round replay would need round history on the finish payload — out of scope per your call.
  • Tie: both bars hit the line together and both flash gold.
  • 20 players: bars thin out but fit; or top 10 race and the rest fade in below.

Option D — The Slam

~8s cheapest to ship

The current screen's language, turned up to eleven. Title cards stamp in with a screen shake ("AND YOUR" / "CHAMPION IS…"), three dots pulse like a heartbeat… then the name slams in full-screen with a white flash and double confetti, docks upward, and the standings stamp in row by row beneath it.

  • Beats: 0.3s + 1s title stamps → 2s heartbeat dots → 3.3s flash + full-screen slam + count-up → 5.3s hero docks, standings stamp in staggered → 7s Play again rises.
  • Sound: a thunk per title stamp, heartbeat under the dots, reveal sting + crowd on the slam, small ticks as rows stamp.
  • Tie: both names slam in stacked, slightly smaller — the flash and crown are shared.
  • 20 players: standings become two columns (or top 8 + "…12 more on your phones").
  • Build: smallest — same layout as today, new timeline; closest evolution of the existing component.

Option E — Roll Call

~10s everyone gets named

The full countdown, last place first. Quick beats for the bottom of the table — each name flashes center stage and files into the standings — then the brakes come on: 3rd gets a moment, 2nd gets a drumroll, the lights drop for a long "…" — and the champion detonates. Every single player hears their name on the big screen.

  • Beats: 8th→4th at ~0.6s each (accelerating) → 3.5s bronze card, held → 5s silver card, dim + drumroll → 6.6s deep-dim "…" heartbeat → 8.1s flash, fireworks, gold card + count-up, top slot glows.
  • Sound: a tick per quick name, rising drumroll from 2nd place, silence in the "…" hold (that's the gasp), full fanfare on the champion.
  • Tie: the champion beat lands two gold cards side by side.
  • 20 players: ranks below 8th go by in a fast flurry (batched "15th–20th" beats) so runtime stays ~12s; everyone still lands on the list, which scrolls or splits into two columns.
  • Most ceremonial of the five — and the most Jackbox: the reveal honors the whole room, not just the winner.
